NAND and NOR are universal gates. Important laws:

A+0=A; A·1=A; A+1=1; A·0=0; A+A̅=1; A·A̅=0

De Morgan: (A+B)̅=A̅B̅ and (AB)̅=A̅+B̅.

5.3 LEDs and Seven-Segment Display

An LED emits light under forward bias. A seven-segment display uses seven LED segments, a–g, to display decimal digits.

In common-cathode displays the cathodes are common; in common-anode displays the anodes are common. Digital ICs process discrete logic states; analog ICs process continuously varying signals.

5.4 IC 7805, IC 7912, Multimeter and CRO

IC 7805: fixed positive regulator providing approximately +5 V under suitable conditions. IC 7912: fixed negative regulator providing approximately −12 V under suitable conditions.

A multimeter measures quantities such as voltage, current and resistance when correctly configured. A CRO displays electrical waveforms and allows amplitude, time period and frequency measurement.

f=1/T
Always select the correct meter function, range and connection before measurement.

5.5 Sensors, Transducers and Power System

A sensor detects a physical quantity such as temperature, light, pressure or displacement. A transducer converts a physical quantity/energy into another form, commonly an electrical signal for measurement/control.

ExampleQuantity
Thermistor/RTDTemperature
LDR/photodiodeLight
Strain-based sensorForce/pressure

Basic power-system path: generation → transmission → distribution → utilization. High-voltage transmission reduces current for a given power and therefore helps reduce resistive I²R losses.

5.6 Electrical Safety and Protective Devices

  • Isolate supply before working.
  • Use correctly rated conductors and protective devices.
  • Never touch exposed energized conductors.
  • Use suitable insulated tools and supervised laboratory procedures.
  • Verify connections before energizing.
DeviceFunction
FuseMelts and opens circuit under excessive current.
MCBResettable protection against specified overload/short circuit conditions.
MCCBHigher-capacity protective device for many distribution/industrial applications.

5.7 Earthing

Earthing provides a low-impedance path for fault current and helps limit dangerous touch voltage when used with a properly designed protection system.

Importance

  • Improves shock protection.
  • Provides a fault-current path for protective-device operation.
  • Helps protect equipment.
  • Supports stable electrical-system operation.

Approved electrode arrangements can include rod, pipe or plate systems depending on installation requirements and applicable standards.

Mains electrical work and earthing installation must be performed by qualified personnel according to applicable safety standards.
