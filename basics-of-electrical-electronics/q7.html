Assignment-1 · Question 7 (Sub-BEEE · Submission: 21/10/2026)

Determine the value of the current through the diode circuit given below. Assume Vγ = 0.7 V.

1. Given Circuit Diagram (From Assignment-1 Sheet)

2. Step-by-Step Numerical Solution

Given Data:

  • DC Supply Voltage, V = 6 V
  • Series Resistance, R = 10 kΩ = 10 × 103 Ω = 10,000 Ω
  • Cut-in (Knee / Barrier) Voltage of Silicon Diode, Vγ = 0.7 V

Step 1 — Check the Biasing Condition of the Silicon Diode:
The positive (+) terminal of the 6 V DC source is connected (via the 10 kΩ resistor) to the p-side (anode) of the Silicon diode, and the negative (−) terminal is connected to the n-side (cathode). Since the applied voltage V = 6 V > Vγ = 0.7 V, the Silicon diode is Forward Biased (ON) and conducts current clockwise around the loop.

Step 2 — Apply Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL) Around the Closed Loop:
Replacing the conducting Silicon diode with its barrier potential drop Vγ = 0.7 V and applying KVL clockwise:

+V − (I × R) − Vγ = 0

6 − I × (10 × 103) − 0.7 = 0

I × (10 × 103) = 6 − 0.7 = 5.3 V

Step 3 — Calculate the Diode Current (I):

I = 5.3 / (10 × 103) A = 0.53 × 10−3 A = 0.53 mA (or 530 μA)

Final Answer:
• Current through the diode circuit, I = 0.53 mA (5.3 × 10−4 A)
• Voltage drop across the 10 kΩ resistor, VR = I × R = 0.53 mA × 10 kΩ = 5.3 V
• Power dissipated across the Silicon diode, PD = Vγ × I = 0.7 V × 0.53 mA = 0.371 mW
