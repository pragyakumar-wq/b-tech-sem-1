Unit 1: Basic Electrical Circuits (8 hrs)

  • 1.1 Resistance, inductance, capacitance as active and passive elements
  • 1.2 Unilateral and bilateral, linear and nonlinear elements
  • 1.3 Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's current and voltage laws
  • 1.4 Series and parallel circuits, voltage and current sources, source transformation
  • 1.5 Mesh analysis and nodal analysis
  • 1.6 Current divider rule, voltage divider rule, star-delta transformation

Unit 2: A.C. Circuits (8 hrs)

  • 2.1 Signals and waveforms
  • 2.2 Instantaneous, peak, average and RMS values
  • 2.3 Peak factor and form factor
  • 2.4 Phase difference, phasors
  • 2.5 Impedance, admittance, power factor, power triangle
  • 2.6 Three-phase systems: necessity, advantages, phase sequence, balanced and unbalanced

Unit 3: Basic Electrical Machines (8 hrs)

  • 3.1 Electromagnetic principles, self and mutual inductance
  • 3.2 DC machines: principle, construction, classification, applications
  • 3.3 Single-phase transformers: working, construction, EMF equation
  • 3.4 Voltage ratio, current ratio, voltage regulation
  • 3.5 Single-phase and three-phase induction motors
  • 3.6 Applications of induction and fractional horsepower motors

Unit 4: Semiconductor Devices (8 hrs)

  • 4.1 Intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors
  • 4.2 p-n junction diode: construction, forward and reverse bias
  • 4.3 Temperature effects and breakdown mechanisms
  • 4.4 Half-wave and full-wave rectifiers, filters, Zener diode as voltage regulator
  • 4.5 BJT: PNP and NPN, construction, operating principle
  • 4.6 CE configuration as amplifier, transistor as a switch

Unit 5: Digital, Analog Electronics, Measurement and Electrical Safety (10 hrs)

  • 5.1 Binary, octal, hexadecimal number systems, binary addition
  • 5.2 Logic gates, universal gates, Boolean algebra
  • 5.3 LEDs, seven-segment display, truth table, digital and analog ICs
  • 5.4 IC 7805 and IC 7912, multimeter and CRO
  • 5.5 Sensors and transducers, generation, transmission and distribution of electrical power
  • 5.6 Electrical safety, fuse, MCB, MCCB
  • 5.7 Earthing systems
