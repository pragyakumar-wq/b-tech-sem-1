3.1 Values in Human-Human Relationship & Justice (Nyaya) (TSO 3a, 3b)

Four Key Aspects of Human Relationship:

  1. Relationship IS — It already exists between one Self ('I1') and another Self ('I2'); we do not have to "create" it, only understand it.
  2. There are definite Feelings (Bhava / Values) in the Self ('I1') for the other Self ('I2').
  3. These feelings can be clearly recognized — they are 9 definite, universal feelings.
  4. Fulfilling these feelings leads to Mutual Happiness (Ubhay-Tripti), which is called Justice (Nyaya).

Definition of Justice (Nyaya): Justice is the recognition of values (the 9 definite feelings) in human-human relationship, their fulfilment, right evaluation of the fulfilment, resulting in Mutual Happiness (Ubhay-Tripti).

The Nine Universal Values (Feelings) in Human Relationships (TSO 3a)

S. No.Value (English / Hindi)Exact Definition & Role in Relationship
1Trust (Vishwas)
Foundational Value (Aadhar Mulya)		To be assured that each human being inherently wants to make oneself and the other happy and prosperous. Foundation of every relationship.
2Respect (Samman)Right Evaluation (Sahi Mulyankan) — evaluating a human being as they truly are (as a Self similar to me in purpose, program, and potential).
3Affection (Sneha)The feeling of being related to the other (accepting the other as one's relative/kin). Naturally flows only when Trust and Respect are present.
4Care (Mamta)The feeling of responsibility and commitment for the nurturing and protection of the Body of one's relative.
5Guidance (Vatsalya)The feeling of responsibility and commitment for ensuring Right Understanding and Right Feelings in the Self ('I') of one's relative.
6Reverence (Shraddha)The feeling of acceptance for Excellence (Shreshthata) — honoring someone who has achieved Right Understanding and lives in harmony at all 4 levels.
7Glory (Gaurav)The feeling for someone who has made efforts for Excellence (contributing sincerely toward human welfare and harmony).
8Gratitude (Kritagyata)The feeling of acceptance for those who have made efforts in my excellence (helped me in developing Right Understanding, feelings, or physical care).
9Love (Prem)
Complete Value (Purna Mulya)		The feeling of being related to ALL (Unconditional & Universal Affection). Starts in the family and expands to the entire world family (Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam).

Deep Dive 1: Trust (Vishwas) — Intention (Chahana) vs. Competence (Yogyata)

To understand Trust clearly, examine these four self-reflective questions:

  • 1a. Do I want to make myself happy? → Yes (Natural Acceptance / Intention)
  • 2a. Do I want to make the other happy? → Yes (Natural Acceptance / Intention)
  • 3a. Does the other want to make himself/herself happy? → Yes (Intention)
  • 4a. Does the other want to make me happy? → Yes (Intention, though we often doubt this!)

Why Relationship Breakdowns Happen: When the other person makes a mistake, we judge them by their lack of Competence (Yogyata), but we judge ourselves by our pure Intention (Chahana)! We say, "I made a mistake by accident, but he did it on purpose!"
True Trust means never doubting the other person's Intention (Natural Acceptance), while realistically assessing their Competence and helping them improve with patience and compassion.

Deep Dive 2: Respect (Samman) vs. Disrespect (Apman) & Differentiation (Bhedbhav)

Respect = Right Evaluation (Sam + Man). Whenever evaluation is not right, it is Disrespect, which takes four forms:

  • 1. Over-Evaluation (Adhimulyankan): Evaluating for more than what it is (e.g., flattery).
  • 2. Under-Evaluation (Avamulyankan): Evaluating for less than what it is (e.g., belittling someone).
  • 3. Otherwise-Evaluation (Anyatha Mulyankan): Evaluating a conscious human being ('I') as a material object or animal.
  • 4. Differentiation / Discrimination (Bhedbhav): Evaluating human beings on the basis of Body (gender, race, age, physical strength), Physical Facilities (wealth, post, designation), or Beliefs (sect, ideology, isms) instead of the Self ('I').

Basis of Right Evaluation (Minimum Content of Respect — The Other is Similar to Me):

  1. Our Purpose is the same: Both of us want continuous happiness and prosperity (Natural Acceptance).
  2. Our Program is the same: Both of us need to understand and live in harmony at all 4 levels.
  3. Our Potential is the same: Desire, Thought, and Expectation operate continuously in both of us, and both have the capacity for self-exploration.

Complete Content of Respect (Complementarity): Once we see that the other is similar to me in purpose, program, and potential, the only difference is in our current level of Competence:
• If the other has more understanding than me: I am committed to learn from them.
• If I have more understanding than the other: I live with a sense of responsibility to help them develop, without ego or pride.

3.2 Harmony in the Society (Samaj mein Vyavastha) (TSO 3c)

Society is not a battlefield of competing individuals — it is an extension of the family where families live together in mutual fulfilment.

1. The Four Comprehensive Human Goals (Samagra Manav Lakshya)

Goal No.Comprehensive Human GoalLevel of LivingHow It Is Fulfilled
1Right Understanding & Right Feeling (Samadhan)In Every IndividualThrough Humanistic Value Education & Self-Exploration.
2Prosperity (Samriddhi)In Every FamilyThrough right assessment of physical needs and eco-friendly production.
3Fearlessness / Trust (Abhay)In SocietyThrough Justice (Nyaya) in human-to-human relationships instead of fear/coercion.
4Co-existence (Sah-Astitva)In Nature / ExistenceThrough mutual enrichment and cyclic production with the four orders of nature.

2. The Five Dimensions of Human Endeavor (Manaviyan Vyavastha ke 5 Aayam)

To achieve the four Comprehensive Human Goals in society, five interconnected societal systems (dimensions) are required:

DimensionMeaning & ScopeGoal Fulfilled
1. Education – Sanskar
(Shiksha – Sanskar)		Education: Developing Right Understanding of harmony at all 4 levels.
Sanskar: Commitment, preparation, and practice of living in harmony (in thought, behavior, and work).		Right Understanding & Right Feeling (Happiness)
2. Health – Sanyam
(Swasthya – Sanyam)		Sanyam: Self-regulation in 'I' for nurturing, protection, and right utilization of the body.
Health: Harmony among body organs so the body serves as a healthy instrument.		Prosperity & Harmony with Body
3. Production – Work
(Utpadan – Karya)		Work: Physical labor humans perform on the rest of nature.
Production: Physical facilities produced via cyclic (eco-friendly) and mutually enriching processes.		Prosperity in Family & Co-existence with Nature
4. Justice – Preservation
(Nyaya – Suraksha)		Justice: Recognition, fulfilment, and evaluation of human-human values leading to Mutual Happiness.
Preservation: Enrichment, protection, and right utilization of natural resources.		Fearlessness (Trust) in Society & Co-existence in Nature
5. Exchange – Storage
(Vinimaya – Kosh)		Exchange: Sharing/trading physical facilities with a view of mutual fulfilment, not exploitation/profiteering.
Storage: Storing surplus facilities for collective social security and seasonal needs, not hoarding.		Prosperity & Fearlessness in Society

3. From Family Order to World Family Order (Universal Human Order — Sarvabhaum Vyavastha)

Harmony in society naturally expands in concentric circles from the family to the entire planet (Undivided Society / Akhand Samaj):

Family → Family Cluster → Village / Community → Village Cluster → Nation → World Family Order (Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam)
