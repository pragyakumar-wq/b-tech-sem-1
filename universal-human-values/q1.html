Question 1 · Unit 1 (CO1)

What is Self-Exploration? Explain its content and the two-step process (Natural Acceptance and Experiential Validation) with a neat diagram. Also differentiate between Prosperity (Samriddhi) and Wealth (Dhan).

Part A: Meaning, Content & Process of Self-Exploration (TSO 1a)

1. Definition of Self-Exploration (Swa-Anveshan):
Self-Exploration is a process of dialogue between "what I am" (my current desires, thoughts, and assumptions) and "what I really want to be" (my innate Natural Acceptance). It is the method of Value Education where every concept is treated as a proposal to be verified within oneself rather than accepted blindly.

2. Content of Self-Exploration:

  • The Desire / Basic Aspiration: To discover what every human being fundamentally aspires for — namely Continuous Happiness (Nirantar Sukha) and Prosperity (Samriddhi).
  • The Program: To understand the process of fulfilling this aspiration — which is Right Understanding and living in harmony at all four levels (Individual, Family, Society, and Nature/Existence).

3. Two-Step Process of Self-Exploration:

  1. Verification on the Basis of Natural Acceptance (Swatah Swikriti): Asking oneself what is naturally acceptable unconditionally (e.g., Do I naturally want to live in relationship or opposition? In nurture or exploitation?). Natural acceptance is invariant with time, place, and beliefs.
  2. Experiential Validation in Living (Anubhavatmak Satyapana): Living according to the proposal in real life:
    • In behavior with human beings, it leads to Mutual Happiness (Ubhay-Tripti).
    • In work with the rest of nature, it leads to Mutual Prosperity (Ubhay-Samriddhi).

Part B: Difference Between Prosperity (Samriddhi) and Wealth (Dhan) (TSO 1b)

ParameterProsperity (Samriddhi)Wealth (Dhan)
DefinitionThe feeling of having or making available more than required physical facilities.A physical quantity of money, goods, or material assets possessed by a person.
DomainInternal feeling in the Self ('I') based on right assessment + physical competence.Purely in the external material domain.
Assessment of NeedRequires recognizing that physical needs of the Body are limited and quantifiable.Without Right Understanding, the craving for wealth feels infinite.
Mental State & ConductFeels satisfied, generous, and eager to share and nurture others (SSSS).A wealthy person without right understanding still feels deprived and insecure (SSDD).
