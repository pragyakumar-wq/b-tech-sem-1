Question 3 · Unit 3 (CO3)

List and explain the nine universal values in human-human relationships. Why is Trust called the foundational value (Intention vs. Competence) and Respect called Right Evaluation? Also explain the Comprehensive Human Goal and its five dimensions.

Part A: Nine Universal Values in Human Relationships (TSO 3a, 3b)

Justice (Nyaya) in relationship is the recognition of 9 universal feelings in the Self ('I'), their fulfilment, and right evaluation leading to Mutual Happiness:

  1. Trust (Vishwas — Foundational Value): Assurance that the other human being inherently intends my happiness and prosperity. We must distinguish between Intention (Natural Acceptance — always pure in everyone) and Competence (current ability to live by it). Doubting intention destroys relationships; evaluating competence realistically builds compassion.
  2. Respect (Samman — Right Evaluation): Evaluating every human being rightly as a Self ('I') equal to me in Purpose (wants continuous happiness), Program (living in harmony at 4 levels), and Potential (continuous desire, thought, expectation) — free from discrimination based on body, wealth, or beliefs.
  3. Affection (Sneha): Feeling of being related to the other as one's kin.
  4. Care (Mamta): Responsibility for nurturing and protecting the Body of one's relative.
  5. Guidance (Vatsalya): Responsibility for developing Right Understanding and Right Feelings in the Self ('I') of one's relative.
  6. Reverence (Shraddha): Acceptance for Excellence (one who understands and lives in harmony at all 4 levels).
  7. Glory (Gaurav): Feeling for those who have made sincere efforts toward Excellence.
  8. Gratitude (Kritagyata): Feeling of acceptance for those who have helped in my own effort toward Excellence.
  9. Love (Prem — Complete Value): Unconditional feeling of being related to ALL human beings (Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam).
