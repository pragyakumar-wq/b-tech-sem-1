5.1 Natural Acceptance of Human Values (TSO 5a)

Why Human Values Are Naturally Acceptable & Universal:

  • Human values (such as Trust, Respect, Affection, Care, Guidance, Non-exploitation, and Co-existence) are not arbitrary moral rules imposed from outside through fear or greed.
  • They emerge directly from the Natural Acceptance (Swatah Swikriti) of every human being because Existence itself is rooted in Co-existence.
  • Because Natural Acceptance is invariant across time, place, culture, and individual, ethical human conduct is universal and naturally joyful rather than a burden or sacrifice.

5.2 Definitiveness of Ethical Human Conduct (Manaviyan Acharan ki Nishchitata) (TSO 5b)

Right Understanding gained through Self-Exploration gives rise to Definite Ethical Human Conduct (Nishchit Manaviyan Acharan), which is the same for all human beings and consists of three core components:

ComponentHindi TermMeaning & Sub-Components
1. ValuesMulyaCompetence of living in accordance with universal human values at all 4 levels:
• Values in Self ('I'): Happiness (Sukha), Peace (Shanti), Satisfaction (Santosh), Bliss (Ananda).
• Values in Human Relationship (9 Values): Trust, Respect, Affection, Care, Guidance, Reverence, Glory, Gratitude, Love.
• Values in Society: Fearlessness (Abhay), Mutual Fulfilment.
• Values in Nature (Utility & Artistic Value): Upayogita Mulya (nurturing/protection of body) and Kala Mulya (preserving/enhancing form and accessibility).
2. PolicyNitiThe decision (plan, program, design, and implementation) for the enrichment, protection, and right utilization of our resources (Self, Body, and Physical Facility):
• 1. Economic Policy (Artha Niti): Policy for enrichment of Mind, Body, and Wealth.
• 2. Political Policy (Rajya Niti): Policy for protection of Mind, Body, and Wealth.
• 3. Policy for Universal Human Order (Dharma Niti): Policy for right utilization of Mind, Body, and Wealth towards universal harmony.
3. CharacterCharitraThe definitiveness of my behavior and work in daily living, expressed through three pillars:
• 1. Chastity in conjugal relationship (Swa-Nari / Swa-Purush): Faithfulness and sanctity in marital relationship.
• 2. Rightfully acquired wealth (Swa-Dhan): Wealth earned through one's own rightful physical labor or received as a voluntary gift in relationship — zero theft or exploitation.
• 3. Compassionate behavior & work (Dayapurna Karya-Vyavahar): Behavior and work that nurture both human beings and nature without violence or exploitation.

5.3 Basis for Humanistic Education, Humanistic Constitution & Universal Order (TSO 5c)

1. Humanistic Education (Manaviyan Shiksha)

Education that ensures the transition from Animal Consciousness to Human Consciousness by integrating:

  • Value Education: Understanding What to do and Why to do (Harmony at all 4 levels).
  • Technical / Skill Education: Learning How to implement holistic, eco-friendly, and people-friendly technologies and systems.

2. Humanistic Constitution (Manaviyan Samvidhan)

A framework of social governance rooted in Right Understanding and Justice rather than mere fear of punishment, addressing:

  • What are fundamental human rights and duties towards family, society, and nature?
  • How do communities self-govern from family to village to world family (Sarvabhaum Vyavastha)?

5.4 Competence in Professional Ethics & Holistic Technologies (TSO 5d)

What is Profession?
In the light of UHV, a Profession is NOT merely a way to earn money or compete for status — it is a mode of participation by an individual in the larger societal order (particularly through Production–Work, Education, Health, or Exchange).

Salient Features of Competence in Professional Ethics:

  1. Clarity about the Comprehensive Human Goal (Samadhan, Samriddhi, Abhay, Sah-Astitva).
  2. Confidence in oneself and in the natural harmony of existence.
  3. Competence of mutually fulfilling behavior with colleagues, clients, and society.
  4. Competence of mutually enriching interaction with Nature (assessing physical needs rightly and using eco-friendly processes).
  5. Competence of actualizing one's understanding in real life without succumbing to unethical pressures.

Prevailing Issues in Professional Ethics vs. Why External Checks Alone Fail

  • Common Unethical Practices Today: Corruption in multiple forms, tax evasion, misappropriation of funds, adulteration and spurious products, cut-throat competition, misleading advertisements, cyber-crime, whistle-blowing dilemmas, and environmental degradation.
  • Why Oaths, Codes of Conduct, Audits & Strict Laws Alone Are Insufficient: External vigilance, CCTV cameras, and stricter punishments try to control behavior from the outside while the person's internal assumption remains "More wealth at any cost = Happiness." Unless internal competence is developed through Value Education (Right Understanding), people find loopholes in external laws.

5.5 Strategy for Transition Towards Universal Human Order (TSO 5d)

(a) Transition at the Level of the Individual

  1. Self-Exploration & Self-Awareness: Continuously observing one's own desires, thoughts, and expectations on the basis of Natural Acceptance.
  2. Living in Harmony: Practicing Sanyam with the Body, fulfilling the 9 values in family relationships, and assessing physical needs rightly.
  3. Socially & Ecologically Responsible Engineering: Choosing career paths, designs, and projects that promote human welfare and environmental preservation.

(b) Transition at the Level of the Society

  1. Promoting Mass Awareness in Value Education: Integrating UHV across schools, universities, and professional institutions.
  2. Developing Holistic Models: Creating living prototypes of eco-friendly production, cooperative management, and community healthcare.
  3. Evolving Policies & Institutions: Reforming economic, educational, and legal frameworks to support the 5 dimensions of Universal Human Order.
