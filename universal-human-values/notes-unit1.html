Self-Exploration, Happiness and Prosperity

1.1 Value Education & The Process of Self-Exploration (TSO 1a)

What is Value Education?
The value of any unit in existence is its participation in the larger order of which it is a part. For example, the value of a pen is that it helps in writing; the value of a human being is participation towards mutual fulfilment (harmony) at all levels of living — within oneself, in family, in society, and in nature/existence.

Why Value Education is Needed Alongside Technical Education:

  • Value Domain (What to do & Why to do): Helps us decide our goals, priorities, and what is truly valuable for human happiness and welfare.
  • Skill Domain (How to do): Teaches science, technology, and management to achieve those goals. Without Value Education, technical skills alone can lead to stress, relationship conflicts, and ecological depletion.

Basic Guidelines for Value Education

  • 1. Universal (Sarvabhaum): Applicable to all human beings across all times, places, castes, creeds, genders, and nationalities.
  • 2. Rational (Tarkasangata): Based on reason and open to logical inquiry, not blind belief, dogma, or set of do's and don'ts.
  • 3. Natural & Verifiable (Swabhavik evam Janchne Yogya): Naturally acceptable to every human being and experientially verifiable in daily living.
  • 4. All-Encompassing (Sarvangina): Covers all dimensions of human life (thought, behavior, work, and realization) and all four levels of living (individual, family, society, and nature).
  • 5. Leading to Harmony (Samarasata): Promotes harmony within the individual and mutual fulfilment with other humans and nature.

Self-Exploration (Swa-Anveshan): Definition, Content & Process (TSO 1a)

Definition: Self-Exploration is the process of investigating within ourselves to discover what is truly valuable to us by observing our own internal feelings, thoughts, and natural acceptance, and validating it in our real-life interactions.

Two Core Contents of Self-Exploration:

  1. The Desire / Goal (Basic Human Aspiration): What is my basic aspiration as a human being? (Continuous Happiness and Prosperity).
  2. The Program (Process to Achieve the Goal): What is the program to fulfill this basic aspiration? (Understanding and living in harmony at all 4 levels: Individual, Family, Society, and Nature/Existence).

1.2 Continuous Happiness and Prosperity (TSO 1b)

Happiness (Sukha)

Definition: "The state or situation in which I live, if there is harmony / synergy in it, then it is naturally acceptable to me to be in that state/situation."

To be in a state of harmony / liking is Happiness (Sukha).

  • Unhappiness (Dukha): To be forced to be in a state of contradiction, conflict, or disharmony is Unhappiness.
  • Continuous Happiness: Being in harmony within oneself and with family, society, and nature at all times without interruption.

Prosperity (Samriddhi)

Definition: "Prosperity is the feeling of having or making available more than required physical facilities."

Two Essential Requirements for Prosperity:

  1. Right Assessment of Physical Needs: Correctly identifying how much physical facility (food, clothes, shelter, instruments) is actually needed for nurturing, protection, and right utilization of the body.
  2. Competence to Produce / Make Available More Than Required: Having the skills and production capacity to generate more than that limited need.

Difference Between Prosperity (Samriddhi) and Wealth (Dhan)

Basis of ComparisonWealth (Dhan)Prosperity (Samriddhi)
NatureWealth is a physical thing — money or quantity of physical facilities accumulated.Prosperity is an internal feeling of having more than required physical facilities.
Limit / BoundWhen physical needs are not assessed, the desire for wealth appears unlimited.Based on a definite, limited assessment of physical needs of the body.
Behavior Towards OthersA person with huge wealth who feels deprived (Daridra) tries to exploit or hoard more from others.A prosperous person feels sufficient and naturally wants to share and nurture others.
Relationship with HappinessHaving wealth alone does not guarantee happiness or satisfaction.Prosperity is an essential part of continuous human happiness and family harmony.

Prevailing False Notions of Happiness & Prosperity

  • Confusing Excitement / Sensory Pleasure with Happiness: Eating a favorite sweet or watching a movie gives temporary sensory pleasure through the Body, not continuous happiness in the Self. Over-indulgence leads to boredom or ill health.
  • Assuming Unlimited Physical Facility = Happiness: Believing that accumulating more money, luxury cars, or gadgets will automatically solve internal stress and relationship conflicts. This leads to the trap of consumerism, competition, and environmental destruction.

1.3 Right Understanding (Sahi Samajh) (TSO 1b)

What is Right Understanding?
Right Understanding means understanding the harmony at all four levels of our existence:

  1. Harmony in the Human Being (Self & Body): Understanding the needs and activities of the sentient Self ('I') and material Body.
  2. Harmony in the Family: Understanding human-to-human relationships and the 9 universal feelings (Trust, Respect, etc.).
  3. Harmony in the Society: Understanding the Comprehensive Human Goal and the 5 dimensions of Universal Human Order.
  4. Harmony in Nature / Existence: Understanding the interconnectedness of the 4 orders of nature and co-existence in space.

Why Right Understanding has the Highest Priority: Without Right Understanding, we neither know how to fulfill relationships (leading to conflict) nor know how much physical facility is enough (leading to deprivation despite wealth). Thus, Right Understanding is the foundation for both Continuous Happiness and Prosperity.
