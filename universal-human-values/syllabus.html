Unit 1: Self-Exploration, Happiness and Prosperity (5 hrs) — Mapped to CO1

Body of Knowledge (BoK):

  • 1.1 Self-Exploration
  • 1.2 Continuous Happiness and Prosperity
  • 1.3 Right Understanding

Theory Session Outcomes (TSOs):

  • TSO 1a: Explain the process of Self-Exploration as the process for Value Education.
  • TSO 1b: Explain Continuous Happiness and Prosperity through Self-Exploration and Right Understanding.

Unit 2: Relationship, Physical Facility and Human Being (5 hrs) — Mapped to CO2

Body of Knowledge (BoK):

  • 2.1 Right Understanding, Relationship and Physical Facility
  • 2.2 Happiness and Prosperity
  • 2.3 Human being as a co-existence of the sentient 'Self' and the material 'Body'

Theory Session Outcomes (TSOs):

  • TSO 2a: Explain the salient features of Right Understanding, Relationship and Physical Facility.
  • TSO 2b: Explain Happiness and Prosperity in the given situation.
  • TSO 2c: Explain human being as a co-existence of the sentient 'Self' and the material 'Body'.

Unit 3: Values in Human Relationships and Harmony in Society (5 hrs) — Mapped to CO3

Body of Knowledge (BoK):

  • 3.1 Values in human-human relationship
  • 3.2 Harmony in the Society

Theory Session Outcomes (TSOs):

  • TSO 3a: List the values in human-human relationship.
  • TSO 3b: Explain the importance of values in establishing harmonious human relationships.
  • TSO 3c: Explain the process to establish Harmony in the given society.

Unit 4: Harmony in Nature and Existence (6 hrs) — Mapped to CO4

Body of Knowledge (BoK):

  • 4.1 Harmony in the Nature
  • 4.2 Interconnectedness and mutual fulfilment among the four orders of nature
  • 4.3 Existence as Co-existence of mutually interacting units in all-pervasive space
  • 4.4 Holistic perception of harmony at all levels of existence

Theory Session Outcomes (TSOs):

  • TSO 4a: Explain Harmony in Nature for the given situation.
  • TSO 4b: Identify the four orders of nature with respect to mutual fulfilment.
  • TSO 4c: Explain Existence as Co-existence of mutually interacting units in all-pervasive space.
  • TSO 4d: Explain holistic perception of harmony at all levels of existence.

Unit 5: Implications of Holistic Understanding on Professional Ethics (7 hrs) — Mapped to CO5

Body of Knowledge (BoK):

  • 5.1 Natural acceptance of human values
  • 5.2 Definitiveness of Ethical Human Conduct
  • 5.3 Basis for Humanistic Education, Humanistic Constitution and Humanistic Universal Order
  • 5.4 Competence in professional ethics
  • 5.5 Strategy for transition from the present state to Universal Human Order: (a) At the level of individual, (b) At the level of society

Theory Session Outcomes (TSOs):

  • TSO 5a: Explain the natural acceptance of human values.
  • TSO 5b: Explain Definitiveness of Ethical Human Conduct.
  • TSO 5c: Explain the basis for Humanistic Education, Humanistic Constitution and Humanistic Universal Order.
  • TSO 5d: Explain competence in professional ethics and strategies for transition towards Universal Human Order at individual and society levels.
