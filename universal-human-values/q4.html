Part B: Existence as Co-existence (Units Submerged in All-Pervasive Space) (TSO 4c)

Existence = Units (Nature) Submerged in All-Pervasive Space (Shunya):

  • Units (Prakriti): Limited in size, active (Kriyashil), energized, self-organized, and mutually fulfilling. Divided into Material (Jada) and Conscious (Chaitanya) units.
  • Space (Shunya / Vyapak): Unlimited/all-pervasive, no activity (Kriya-shunya), constant equilibrium energy, transparent, and permeating every unit.
  • Because all units are submerged in Space, every unit is energized, self-organized, and recognizes & fulfills its relationship with every other unit.
