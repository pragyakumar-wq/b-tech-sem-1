Question 5 · Unit 5 (CO5)

What is meant by Definitiveness of Ethical Human Conduct (Values, Policy, and Character)? Discuss competence in professional ethics, criteria for holistic technologies and production systems, and strategies for transition to a Universal Human Order.

Part A: Definitiveness of Ethical Human Conduct (TSO 5b)

Right Understanding naturally manifests as Definite Ethical Human Conduct (Nishchit Manaviyan Acharan), which is invariant and universal across all human beings and comprises three pillars:

  1. Values (Mulya): Competence of living in accordance with universal human values at the levels of Self (Happiness, Peace, Satisfaction, Bliss), Family (9 relationship values from Trust to Love), Society (Fearlessness), and Nature (Utility Value & Artistic Value).
  2. Policy (Niti): Decision and plan for the enrichment (Artha Niti), protection (Rajya Niti), and right utilization (Dharma Niti) of Mind, Body, and Wealth.
  3. Character (Charitra): Definite outer conduct characterized by: (i) Swa-Nari / Swa-Purush (chastity in marital life), (ii) Swa-Dhan (rightfully earned wealth through one's own labor), and (iii) Dayapurna Karya-Vyavahar (compassionate behavior and work without exploitation).

Part B: Competence in Professional Ethics, Holistic Technologies & Transition Strategy (TSO 5d)

  • Competence in Professional Ethics: Viewing profession as meaningful participation in the larger societal order. Ensures clarity of the Comprehensive Human Goal, mutually fulfilling workplace relationships, and eco-friendly production without corruption or exploitation.
  • Criteria for Holistic Technologies & Production Systems:
    1. Catering to genuine human needs rather than artificial greed.
    2. Utilizing cyclic (Avartanshil), renewable, and biodegradable natural resources.
    3. Ensuring mutual enrichment of humans and all four orders of nature (eco-friendly and people-friendly).
  • Transition Strategy Towards Universal Human Order:
    • At the Individual Level: Practicing self-exploration, living in harmony within family and nature, and acting as a socially and ecologically responsible engineer.
    • At the Societal Level: Universalizing Humanistic Value Education, building living models of holistic production and cooperative management, and aligning public policies with the 5 dimensions of Universal Human Order.
