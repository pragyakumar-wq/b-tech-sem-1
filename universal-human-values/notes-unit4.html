4.1 Harmony in the Nature: Self-Regulation & Recyclability (TSO 4a)

What is Nature (Prakriti)?
The aggregate (collection) of all mutually interacting units — big or small, sentient or material — together is called Nature (Prakriti). Despite having infinite units, all units of Nature can be classified into Four Distinct Orders.

Two Fundamental Features of Harmony in Nature:

  • 1. Recyclability (Avartanshilata): Natural processes are cyclic and self-renewing (e.g., water cycle, carbon-oxygen cycle, nitrogen cycle, soil-plant decomposition cycle). The output/waste of one unit becomes nutrient/input for another unit, so nothing is wasted in Nature.
  • 2. Self-Regulation (Swa-Niyantran): Nature regulates populations, proportions, and climatic balances automatically without needing external management (e.g., in a forest, soil fertility is maintained, seeds germinate, and predator-prey populations stay balanced naturally).

4.2 Interconnectedness & Mutual Fulfilment Among the Four Orders of Nature (TSO 4b)

Every unit in Nature belongs to one of the Four Orders of Nature, which exist in a relationship of Paraspara-Purakata (Interconnectedness & Mutual Fulfilment):

  1. Material / Physical Order (Padartha Avastha): Soil, water, air, rocks, metals, minerals, planets, stars.
  2. Pranic / Bio / Plant Order (Prana Avastha): Grass, plants, trees, herbs, shrubs, cellular flora.
  3. Animal Order (Jiva Avastha): Birds, insects, aquatic life, land animals (Co-existence of Self 'I' + Animal Body).
  4. Human / Knowledge Order (Gyana Avastha): Human beings (Co-existence of Self 'I' with potential for Knowledge + Human Body).

How the First Three Orders Fulfill Each Other vs. The Human Problem

  • First Three Orders (Material, Pranic, Animal): Already live in complete mutual fulfilment! Soil, water, and air nurture plants; decaying plants enrich soil and prevent erosion; plants provide oxygen and food to animals and humans; animal waste enriches soil and aids seed dispersal.
  • The Human Order Today: Due to lack of Right Understanding, humans have not yet fulfilled their role toward the other three orders — instead causing resource depletion, pollution, deforestation, and species extinction.
  • Solution: When human beings awaken to Right Understanding (Gyana), they exhibit Dhirata (perseverance in understanding truth), Virata (bravery in helping others understand), and Udarata (generosity in investing mind, body, and wealth for mutual fulfilment), completing the cycle of harmony across all four orders.

4.3 Existence as Co-existence (Sah-Astitva) of Units in All-Pervasive Space (TSO 4c)

Fundamental Equation of Existence:
Existence (Astitva) = Existential Units (Nature / Prakriti) submerged in All-Pervasive Space (Shunya / Vyapak)

Every unit (material or conscious) is submerged (Bhinga, Ghira, aur Duba hua) in all-pervasive Space. Because every unit is submerged in Space, every unit is: (1) Energized in Space, (2) Self-organized in Space, and (3) Recognizes its relationship with every other unit in Space and fulfills it.

Key Takeaway of Holistic Perception: There is no inherent conflict anywhere in Existence! From the smallest atom to planetary systems, and from the individual Self ('I') to Family, Society, and Nature, Co-existence (Sah-Astitva) and Harmony are already built into the design of Existence. Human beings only need to understand this harmony (Realization & Understanding) and live in alignment with it.
