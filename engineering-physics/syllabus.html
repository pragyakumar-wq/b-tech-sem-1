Unit 1: Wave Optics (8 hrs)

  • 1.1 Interference by division of amplitude, Newton's rings, Michelson interferometer
  • 1.2 Fraunhofer diffraction: single slit, double slit, circular aperture
  • 1.3 Diffraction grating
  • 1.4 Rayleigh criterion, resolving power of telescope
  • 1.5 Polarization, double-refracting crystal, Nicol prism

Unit 2: Lasers and Optical Fibre (8 hrs)

  • 2.1 Characteristics of laser light, Einstein's A and B coefficients, population inversion, pumping, optical resonator
  • 2.2 Ruby laser, He-Ne laser, semiconductor laser
  • 2.3 Structure of optical fibre, TIR, acceptance angle, numerical aperture, types of fibres
  • 2.4 Propagation of light in fibre, attenuation
  • 2.5 Applications of optical fibres, fibre optic sensors

Unit 3: Electromagnetism and Propagation of Waves (8 hrs)

  • 3.1 Electric fields, Gauss' law, dielectrics and capacitors
  • 3.2 Magnetic fields and magnetic materials
  • 3.3 Maxwell's equations
  • 3.4 Electromagnetic waves and Poynting theorem

Unit 4: Quantum Mechanics (8 hrs)

  • 4.1 Introduction to quantum mechanics
  • 4.2 De-Broglie hypothesis, Heisenberg uncertainty principle
  • 4.3 Wave function and its characteristics
  • 4.4 Schrodinger's equation (time-dependent and independent)
  • 4.5 Particle in a box, energy eigenvalues and eigenfunctions
  • 4.6 Potential barrier and tunneling

Unit 5: Semiconductors and Nano-materials (10 hrs)

  • 5.1 Types of materials (metal, semiconductor, insulator)
  • 5.2 Intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors, p-type and n-type, diffusion, drift, Fermi level
  • 5.3 Photodiode, p-n junction transistor, LED
  • 5.4 Hall effect
  • 5.5 Solar cell and its characteristics
  • 5.6 Nanoscience and nanotechnology, unique properties, bulk vs nanomaterials
  • 5.7 Classification of nanostructures: 0D (quantum dot), 1D (nanowire) and more
