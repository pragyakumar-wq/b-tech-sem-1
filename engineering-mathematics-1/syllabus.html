Unit 1: Basic Linear Algebra (8 hrs)

  • 1.1 Elementary transformations and rank of a matrix (echelon and normal form)
  • 1.2 Linear dependence and independence of vectors in Rn
  • 1.3 Consistency of a system of linear equations
  • 1.4 Eigenvalues and eigenvectors, Cayley-Hamilton theorem
  • 1.5 Similarity and diagonalization of square matrices
  • 1.6 Quadratic form, canonical form of matrices

Unit 2: Differential Calculus: Single Variable (8 hrs)

  • 2.1 Higher order derivatives, successive differentiation, Leibnitz's theorem
  • 2.2 Mean value theorems (Rolle's, Lagrange's, Cauchy)
  • 2.3 Indeterminate forms, L'Hopital rule
  • 2.4 Tangents and normals of algebraic and polar curves, curvature

Unit 3: Differential Calculus: Several Variables (9 hrs)

  • 3.1 Partial differentiation, Euler's theorems
  • 3.2 Limit, continuity and differentiability of functions of several variables
  • 3.3 Taylor's theorem with various forms of remainders, Maclaurin's theorem
  • 3.4 Maxima and minima for functions of several variables, Lagrange's multiplier method

Unit 4: Integral Calculus (9 hrs)

  • 4.1 Differentiation under the integral sign, Leibnitz's rule
  • 4.2 Double integral, change of order of integration, polar co-ordinates
  • 4.3 Triple integral, spherical polar and cylindrical polar co-ordinates
  • 4.4 Beta, Gamma, elliptic integral, relation between Beta and Gamma functions

Unit 5: Vector Calculus (8 hrs)

  • 5.1 Scalar and vector valued functions, gradient, divergence and curl
  • 5.2 Tangent plane, directional derivative
  • 5.3 Line, surface and volume integrals
  • 5.4 Green's, Stokes' and Gauss divergence theorems
