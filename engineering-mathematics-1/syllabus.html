Unit 1: Basic Linear Algebra (8 hrs) — Mapped to CO1

Body of Knowledge (BoK):

  • 1.1 Elementary transformations and Rank of Matrix, Rank by using Echelon and Normal form of Matrices. Linear dependence & independence of vectors in Rn space.
  • 1.2 Consistency of System of Linear Equations.
  • 1.3 Eigenvalues & Eigenvectors, Cayley-Hamilton Theorem.
  • 1.4 Similarity of Matrices & Diagonalization of Square Matrices. Quadratic Form, Canonical Form of Matrices.

Theory Session Outcomes (TSOs):

  • TSO 1a: Find the rank of a given Matrix by using various methods.
  • TSO 1b: Test the Consistency of a given system of simultaneous linear equations representing an engineering situation.
  • TSO 1c: Apply Cayley-Hamilton Theorem to find the integral powers of a given matrix.
  • TSO 1d: Test the nature of the transformed matrix of a given homogeneous quadratic polynomial.

Unit 2: Differential Calculus: Single Variable (8 hrs) — Mapped to CO2

Body of Knowledge (BoK):

  • 2.1 Higher order derivatives; Successive differentiation and Leibnitz's Theorem.
  • 2.2 Mean Value Theorems (Rolle's; Lagrange's; Cauchy).
  • 2.3 Indeterminate Forms; L'Hopital Rule.
  • 2.4 Tangents & Normal of Algebraic & Polar Curves, Curvature.

Theory Session Outcomes (TSOs):

  • TSO 2a: Find the nth order derivatives of given functions representing an engineering situation.
  • TSO 2b: Apply the Mean Value Theorem to determine the nature of the tangent of given curves under certain conditions.
  • TSO 2c: Evaluate limits of given functions applying L'Hopital Rule.
  • TSO 2d: Find the Curvature of a given curve representing an engineering situation.

Unit 3: Differential Calculus: Several Variables (9 hrs) — Mapped to CO3

Body of Knowledge (BoK):

  • 3.1 Partial differentiations; Euler's theorems.
  • 3.2 Limit, Continuity and differentiability of functions of Several Variables.
  • 3.3 Taylor's theorem with various forms of remainders, Maclaurin's theorem and related problems.
  • 3.4 Maxima & Minima for functions of several variables; Lagrange's Multiplier Method.

Theory Session Outcomes (TSOs):

  • TSO 3a: Apply Euler's theorem on homogeneous functions of m independent variables of degree n.
  • TSO 3b: Test the differentiability of a given function of several variables.
  • TSO 3c: Apply Taylor's Theorem to expand a function representing an engineering situation in the neighborhood of a given point.
  • TSO 3d: Optimize a given function by applying Lagrange's Multiplier Method for given engineering problems.

Unit 4: Integral Calculus (9 hrs) — Mapped to CO4

Body of Knowledge (BoK):

  • 4.1 Differentiation under the integral sign; Leibnitz's rule.
  • 4.2 Double integral; Change the order of integration; Evaluation of double integration by changing it into polar co-ordinates.
  • 4.3 Triple Integral; Evaluation of Triple integral by changing to Spherical Polar co-ordinates & Cylindrical polar co-ordinates.
  • 4.4 Beta; Gamma; Elliptic integral; Relation between Beta & Gamma functions, General Properties and related problems.

Theory Session Outcomes (TSOs):

  • TSO 4a: Evaluate the given definite integral using Leibnitz's rule.
  • TSO 4b: Find the area of the given plane figure by using double integration.
  • TSO 4c: Find the Volume of the given 3-D figure by using triple integration.
  • TSO 4d: Evaluate given improper integrals by using Beta & Gamma functions.

Unit 5: Vector Calculus (8 hrs) — Mapped to CO5

Body of Knowledge (BoK):

  • 5.1 Scalar and vector valued functions; Concepts of gradient, divergence and curl and their geometrical and physical significance; tangent plane; directional derivative.
  • 5.2 Line, surface and volume integrals - Statement of Green's, Stokes' and Gauss divergence theorems - verification and evaluation of vector integrals using them.

Theory Session Outcomes (TSOs):

  • TSO 5a: Find the divergence and curl of a given vector point function representing a physical quantity.
  • TSO 5b: Apply Stokes' theorem to convert given line integral into surface integral.
  • TSO 5c: Apply Gauss' theorem to convert given surface integral into volume integral.
