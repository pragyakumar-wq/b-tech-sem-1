Question 1

Define Artificial Intelligence (AI). Explain the history and evolution of AI, its foundations, and major application domains with suitable examples.

Definition of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that deals with the development of machines and computer systems that can perform tasks which normally require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, decision-making, understanding natural language, perception, and recognizing patterns.

In simple words, AI enables machines to think, learn, and act intelligently according to the given situation.

Examples: Chatbots, voice assistants, recommendation systems, self-driving vehicles, and face-recognition systems.

History and Evolution of AI

  1. 1940s–1950sEarly Foundations: The mathematical and computational foundations of AI were developed. In 1950, Alan Turing proposed the famous Turing Test to examine whether a machine could demonstrate intelligent behaviour.
  2. 1956Birth of AI: The term Artificial Intelligence was formally introduced at the Dartmouth Conference in 1956. John McCarthy and other researchers proposed AI as a field of research.
  3. 1960s–1970sEarly AI Programs: Researchers developed programs capable of solving mathematical problems, playing games, and processing simple language. Early systems such as ELIZA demonstrated basic language interaction.
  4. 1980sExpert Systems: AI became popular through expert systems, which used knowledge and rules to solve problems in specific domains such as medical diagnosis and finance.
  5. 1990s–2000sMachine Learning: AI increasingly focused on machine learning, where systems learn patterns from data instead of depending only on manually written rules.
  6. 2010sDeep Learning: Advances in computing power, large datasets, and neural networks led to major improvements in image recognition, speech recognition, translation, and other applications.
  7. 2020sGenerative AI: Modern AI systems can generate text, images, audio, video, and computer code. Large language models and generative AI have made AI useful in education, programming, research, business, and many other fields.

Foundations of AI

AI is based on knowledge from several fields:

  • Computer Science: Provides algorithms, programming, data structures, and computational methods.
  • Mathematics: Provides probability, statistics, logic, and optimization techniques.
  • Psychology: Helps in understanding human learning, perception, and decision-making.
  • Neuroscience: Provides inspiration for artificial neural networks.
  • Philosophy: Contributes ideas about knowledge, reasoning, intelligence, and rationality.
  • Linguistics: Helps AI systems understand and process human language.
  • Economics: Provides concepts related to decision-making, utility, and optimization.
  • Control Theory: Helps intelligent systems make decisions and control their actions.

Conclusion

Artificial Intelligence has evolved from simple rule-based programs to advanced learning and generative systems. It combines knowledge from many fields and is now widely used in healthcare, education, finance, transportation, robotics, and everyday applications.
