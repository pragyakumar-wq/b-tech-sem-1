Unit 1: Constitution and Preamble (8 hrs)

  • 1.1 Meaning of the Constitution of India
  • 1.2 Historical perspective
  • 1.3 Salient features and characteristics
  • 1.4 Preamble to the Constitution

Unit 2: Fundamental Rights and Duties (8 hrs)

  • 2.1 Fundamental Rights under Part-III
  • 2.2 Fundamental duties and their significance

Unit 3: Directive Principles (8 hrs)

  • 3.1 Relevance of Directive Principles of State Policy under Part-IV
  • 3.2 Different articles of State policies under Part-IV

Unit 4: Governance (9 hrs)

  • 4.1 Governance under Part-VI
  • 4.2 State Legislature, Legislative Assembly and Legislative Council

Unit 5: Amendments of Constitution (9 hrs)

  • 5.1 Amendment of constitutional powers and procedure
  • 5.2 Major amendments: 42nd, 44th, 74th, 76th, 86th and 91st
